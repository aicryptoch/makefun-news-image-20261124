# THE TRADE WINDS POST — Tuesday 24 November 2026

One newspaper page for one day: Tuesday 24 November 2026, with every
story first reported on Monday 23 or Tuesday 24 November 2026. The page
carries 34 dated stories across 9 regions — Europe 7, Middle East 4,
Africa 1, Americas 9, Asia 2, South Asia 3, Asia-Pacific 3, Oceania 1
and World 4 — as 13 illustrated cards plus 21 one-line briefs.

The lead stories: COP31 ends with no deal, the first failure since
2000, after the Antalya Pact text dropped every mention of fossil
fuels; Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang as CEO and the shares jump
+31%; and the Russian cargo ship Sparta II, hit off the Gulf of
Finland, is towed clear with all 16 crew safe. A verified timeline
strip, a dotted world map whose numbered pins match the story numbers,
and a date-check box naming the older stories that were deliberately
excluded complete the page. Every small card keeps its red REPORTED
credit line in its own footer strip, clear of the story text.
This second-wave edition was rebuilt so those footer strips are
reserved space on every card: no headline, body line or credit can
collide, and each of the 13 cards can be read top to bottom at full
resolution and in the small leaderboard thumbnail alike.

## What is in this repository

- `entry.png` — the finished front page, exactly 1024×1024 px.
- `main.py` — the entrypoint (standard library only). It opens
  `entry.png`, enforces the gate in code (real PNG, exactly
  1024×1024, at most 1 MiB) and writes the PNG bytes out exactly.
- `LICENSE` — the license for this entry.
- `.makefun/freshness.json` — the freshness beacon for this bounty.

Pictures and map: original, drawn in code.
